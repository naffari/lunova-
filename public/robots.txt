# AI crawlers, allowed on purpose.
#
# These are named explicitly rather than left to the wildcard so the decision is
# visible. An increasing share of "who cleans houses in Overland Park" is
# answered inside an assistant rather than on a results page, and a crawler that
# is blocked cannot cite a business it never read. For a local service business
# with nothing proprietary on the site, allowing them costs nothing and blocking
# them means being absent from the answer.
#
# Two distinct jobs, both allowed:
#   - training crawlers  (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended)
#   - live retrieval     (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot,
#                         PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User)
# Retrieval is the one that produces a citation today. Training is the one that
# gets the brand into an answer with no citation at all.
#
# To opt out later, change the relevant Allow to Disallow — do not delete the
# block, or the wildcard above silently re-allows it.
